Visitor Information & Daily Life

What Families Should Know

Practical information for families and visitors.

Visiting Hours

Morning: 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM. Evening: 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM. Visitors are requested to obtain permission and help maintain a peaceful environment for residents.

Meal Times

Meals and beverages are served throughout the day, from morning tea at 6:30 AM to bed milk at 9:00 PM.

Nutritionist Visits

A nutritionist visits daily to help monitor residents' dietary needs.

Health Monitoring

Medical information is maintained from admission to help track health conditions. A GRBS blood sugar test may be conducted by a nurse during a resident's stay.

Boarding & Lodging

Boarding and lodging facilities are provided as part of residential care.

Personal Laundry

Personal laundry is included as part of our care services.
