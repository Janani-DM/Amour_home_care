Our Story

Serving Pondicherry Since 2012

Amour Total Care Home is a non-governmental organisation founded in 2012 in Pondicherry, with a capacity of 40 beds, providing management of both medical and nursing care for older adults and people with mental health challenges.

We have a deep understanding of the needs of seniors and people with mental health challenges. We offer a spectrum of care services, including long-term care, end-of-life care and psychiatric-specific management, with accommodation and services suited to individual needs and preferences.
