Why Your Support Matters

Every Contribution Makes a Difference

Since 2012, Amour Total Care Home has provided a safe, nurturing home for older adults and people with mental health challenges. Many of our residents come to us with little or no family support.

Donations help us keep care compassionate and dignified: nutritious meals, medical and nursing support, medicines and a comfortable place to live.

Where Your Gift Goes

How Your Donation Helps

Daily Meals

Nutritious, freshly prepared meals suited to each resident's health needs.

Medical & Nursing Care

Medicines, check-ups and round-the-clock nursing support for our residents.

A Comfortable Home

Clean rooms, bedding, clothing and a warm, safe place to live with dignity.
